# Spelling List - Week 2 ("ai" and "ay" sounds)
rain, train, paint, snail, wait, day, play, stay, birthday, rainbow.
Tip from class: "ai" usually goes in the MIDDLE of a word, "ay" usually goes at the END.

# Great Sentences
- Start every sentence with a capital letter.
- End with a full stop (.), a question mark (?) or an exclamation mark (!).
- Names of people and places start with a capital letter too.

# Describing Words (Adjectives)
Adjectives tell us more about a noun. "The FLUFFY, WHITE bunny hopped."
Use your five senses: how does it look, sound, smell, feel, taste?

# Story Writing - "Beginning, Middle, End"
- Beginning: who is in the story and where are they?
- Middle: what problem happens?
- End: how is the problem solved?
