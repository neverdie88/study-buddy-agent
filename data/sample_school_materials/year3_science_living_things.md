# Living and Non-Living Things
Living things grow, need food and water, breathe, move, and make new living things.
A rock is non-living. A cat, a tree and a ladybug are living.

# Life Cycle of a Butterfly
1. Egg - a butterfly lays tiny eggs on a leaf.
2. Caterpillar (larva) - it hatches and eats lots of leaves to grow.
3. Chrysalis (pupa) - it makes a hard case and changes inside.
4. Butterfly (adult) - it comes out with wings and can lay eggs again.
Our class remembers it as "Egg, Eat, Wait, Fly!"

# What Plants Need
Plants need sunlight, water, air and soil (nutrients) to grow.
Leaves catch the sunlight to make food for the plant.

# Animal Groups
- Mammals have fur or hair and feed milk to their babies (e.g. bunny, dog).
- Birds have feathers and lay eggs (e.g. parrot).
- Insects have 6 legs and 3 body parts (e.g. bee, butterfly).
- Fish live in water and have gills.
